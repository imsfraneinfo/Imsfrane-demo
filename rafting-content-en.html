OUR SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE

Two days.
A thousand memories.

An immersive journey along the Ahansal River, with a night camping, local meals and opportunities to discover regional heritage.

Two possible endpoints: Sidi Youssef Bridge or continuing to Bin El Ouidane Lake.

Day 01 — The adventure begins

  • Welcome, 4×4 transfer, equipment and briefing
  • Rafting descent from Amougr
  • Lunch and rest stop in Ighrman
  • Visit Aït Hssine and a traditional oil mill
  • Dinner, campfire and overnight camping

Day 02 — Into the gorges

  • Breakfast and return to the water
  • Rafting toward Sidi Youssef Bridge
  • Finish the expedition or continue toward Bin El Ouidane
  • Lake option: boat, lunch and return

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Good to know.

A few important things to know before choosing your route.

Ask a question ↗
When is the rafting season?

Usually from March to June. Depending on the year and water levels, July may also be possible. Each date is confirmed individually.

Is safety equipment provided?

Yes. Our team provides safety equipment, a briefing and a practical introduction before the descent. Please follow your guide's instructions throughout the trip.

Where do we meet?

Depending on the arrangements, we can meet at your accommodation, campsite or another location agreed with our team.

Can the routes change?

Yes. For safety reasons, river flow, weather and on-water conditions may require a route change or cancellation.

How do I confirm my trip?

Send us your preferred route, date and number of participants. We will check the conditions and confirm whether the trip can go ahead.
