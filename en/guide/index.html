IMSFRANE · HAUT ATLAS

Where is Imsfrane?

Imsfrane (also known as Msfrane) is in Tilouguite commune, Azilal province, in Morocco’s Central High Atlas. The mountain is nicknamed “Cathedral” for its rock silhouette; the name does not indicate a religious site.

Location

Tilouguite commune · Azilal province · Morocco.

Landmarks

Jebel Imsfrane, forested valleys, Oued Ahansal and mountain scenery.

Best tip

Search for Tilouguite first, then confirm the exact activity or accommodation meeting point.

Local travel and getting around

Own vehicle, shared taxi or prearranged transfer: plan your arrival around the activity start time.

Driving / rental car

Download the route offline, fuel up before remote stretches and avoid late arrival without prior route knowledge.

Shared grand taxis

Ask locally about Beni Mellal → Ouaouizeght → Tilouguite connections. Schedules, onward rides and fares change.

Private transfer

Share your starting city, date, number of travellers and luggage. A separate transport quote can be prepared.

Meeting point

Rafting, hiking and accommodation may all have different meeting points. Ask for the GPS location before travelling.

When is the best time to visit?

Mountain weather varies widely from year to year. Rafting depends on actual river levels; hiking depends on safe trail conditions.

March – June

Usual rafting season

River levels need checking before every trip. Adapt hikes to the weather.

July – August

Early hikes · MTB · quad

Rafting is sometimes possible in July but never guaranteed. Expect warm daytime conditions.

September – November

Hiking · nature · biking

Shorter days; check trail and road conditions.

December – February

Mountain visits when conditions allow

Cold, possible severe weather and difficult roads: check ahead.

Safety, phone coverage and responsible travel

A few precautions help make your trip safer and protect the local environment.

Weather & river

Storms, flash floods and water levels may change or cancel departures. Never enter a gorge or river without recent local advice.

Signal & navigation

Mobile coverage may be patchy. Keep offline maps and share your plan with someone.

Hiking & quads

Stay on agreed trails, follow guide instructions and respect your abilities and local villages.

Respect nature

Take litter home, avoid picking wild plants and ask before photographing residents.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel without my own car?

Yes, with taxis or a prearranged transfer. Confirm transport connections to Tilouguite and the final journey to Imsfrane before leaving.

Is rafting available year-round?

No. The usual period is March through June, occasionally July if the river and safety conditions allow. Departures are never guaranteed without your guide’s confirmation.

Does the listed price include transport?

Not necessarily. Rates are displayed per group, person, quad or tent/night. Ask for the complete inclusions and total before confirming.

Can a hike start at my accommodation?

Sometimes. This can be arranged depending on where you stay and the selected walk. Agree on the meeting point beforehand.

Are tent prices per person?

No. Prices of 60, 120, 150 and 200 MAD are for a full tent per night, depending on its capacity.

How do I book a custom itinerary?

Send your dates, group size, starting city and preferred activities through the booking form or WhatsApp. The team will confirm availability.
