Wakoudene Waterfalls

Hidden between the villages of Imi N’Ouarg and Tamega, Wakoudene offers fresh pools, wild vegetation and peaceful mountain scenery.

High cliffs, steep slopes and a river shaped over thousands of years create one of the region’s most impressive landscapes.

Tilouguite is an authentic Amazigh village surrounded by valleys, mountains, traditional agriculture and weekly markets.

Flowing from Taghia toward Bin El Ouidane, Oued Ahansal connects villages, valleys and some of the Atlas Mountains’ most beautiful scenery.
