Atlas camping checklist: what to pack

A practical checklist for a mountain night.

A practical checklist for a mountain night.

What to know

A mountain camping bag should be prepared for the night, not just the sunny afternoon. Pack a reliable tent, a sleeping bag suited to forecast temperatures and an insulating mat. Dry spare clothes and a warm layer make a big difference when wind arrives after dark.

Prepare your visit

Include drinking water, simple food, a headlamp with spare power, rain protection, sun protection and essential medication. Keep a basic first-aid kit, rubbish bags and an offline map. Test your tent and stove before travelling instead of learning at the campsite.

Travel responsibly

Ask about the camping location, land permission and nearest safe water supply before departure. The High Atlas can feel remote, so inform a trusted person of the plan. If bad weather is forecast, choose sheltered accommodation rather than an exposed bivouac.

Conditions can change: confirm local information before setting out.
