02 / Our story

From one tent… to a complete adventure.

It began with a simple idea: renting tents to visitors who wanted to spend a night outdoors in Imsfrane. Little by little, that first camping experience grew into new ways to discover our region.

Bicycles came next, followed by rafting and a large mobile camp. Today, we prepare stays combining outdoor activities, nights under canvas and meals for groups. We set up camp from scratch, pitch the tents and organise life on site.

The project has grown, but the spirit remains: working together, sharing the nature of our region and welcoming visitors with local hospitality.
