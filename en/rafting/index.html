Plan your visit to Imsfrane

When can I go rafting on the Ahansal?

The usual season is March to June, occasionally July when river levels allow. Ask us to confirm conditions for your dates before travelling; the route and departure depend on the river.

How do I arrange a trip from Marrakech?

Our base is in the Imsfrane area, in Tilouguite commune, Azilal province. Tell us your starting point and whether you need transport or an overnight stay. We will confirm the meeting point, transfer arrangements and their cost with your programme.

Which rafting programme should I book?

Send your dates, group size and previous rafting experience. We will recommend a suitable route and confirm the duration, price and included services before you book.
