ROUTES & EXPEDITIONS
02

Tilouguite ↔ Imsfrane

Stay in Tilouguite, cycle to Imsfrane and return in the afternoon. An easier outward leg and a more demanding ride back.

24 km outwardAfternoon returnLonger climbs on the return
The routeTilouguite → Imsfrane → Tilouguite
See the itinerary
  1. Spend the night in Tilouguite before setting out by bike towards Imsfrane.
  2. The outward ride is approximately 24 km, with some climbing and descending.
  3. The return involves considerably more climbing before descending. This circuit suits riders looking for a sporting challenge.

Programme and price on request

03

Bivouac & Imsfrane summit

A night in tents, an uphill bike ride and the final approach to the summit on foot.

Bike + hikeApprox. 40 min walking towards the summitGuide + bike guard
The routeImsfrane bivouac → Uphill ride → Forest bike stop → Summit on foot
See the itinerary
  1. Stay in tents at Imsfrane before the outing.
  2. The cycling section is uphill almost throughout, ending at a stopping point in the forest.
  3. Leave the bikes there and continue for approximately 40 minutes on foot towards the summit.
  4. A guard stays with the bikes during the walk. The guard’s cost is part of the arrangements, in addition to the guide’s accompaniment.

Programme and price on request

04

The long traverse via Taghia

A multi-stage itinerary for riders who enjoy longer distances: Zaouiat Ahansal, Taghia and Almou n’Talmest.

Approx. 50 km to Zaouiat AhansalTaghia by bikeDemanding riding
The routeImsfrane → Zaouiat Ahansal → Taghia → Zaouiat Ahansal → Almou n’Talmest → Imsfrane
See the itinerary
  1. Ride approximately 50 km from Imsfrane to Zaouiat Ahansal for an overnight stop.
  2. Continue to Taghia by bike: approximately 7 km further outward, then return to Zaouiat Ahansal.
  3. Take the direction of the Azilal road to reach Almou n’Talmest for another overnight stay.
  4. Return by descending behind the Imsfrane massif. The stage plan is specified in your personalised programme.

Programme and price on request

05

Anergui: return ride or extended loop

Ride from Imsfrane to Anergui, then choose a direct return or an extension via Aït Abdi.

Approx. 40 km to AnerguiTwo alternativesTailored stages
The routeOption 1: Imsfrane ↔ Anergui · Option 2: Anergui → Aït Abdi → Zaouiat Ahansal road → Imsfrane
See the itinerary
  1. First option: approximately 40 km from Imsfrane to Anergui, followed by the return ride.
  2. Second option: continue from Anergui towards Aït Abdi, descend to the Zaouiat Ahansal road and return to Imsfrane.
  3. The stage breakdown and duration depend on your chosen alternative.

Plan your visit to Imsfrane

How much does mountain bike rental cost?

Rental is 150 MAD per bike for exploring Imsfrane, whether you take it for a full day or less. Confirm bike availability, collection and return arrangements with us before setting out.

Can I request a longer Atlas MTB trip?

Yes. Our routes include Tilouguite–Imsfrane, a bivouac and summit outing, the traverse via Zaouiat Ahansal and Taghia, and options towards Anergui. These programmes have no fixed advertised price: request the itinerary and quote on WhatsApp.

What information do you need to plan my ride?

Send your dates, group size, cycling experience and preferred distance. The Tilouguite route has about 24 km on the outward leg, with more climbing on the return; longer itineraries require suitable fitness and agreed logistics.
