Frequently asked questions

How much is a full-day rental?

150 MAD per person for a full day, with one bike per person.

How much is the guided tour?

100 MAD per person for a ride of up to two hours, including an individual bike and a local guide.

Can I rent without a guide?

Yes. The full-day rental is a separate option from the guided tour.

Are routes suitable for everyone?

Route and difficulty are confirmed based on experience and trail conditions.

Wear a helmet and adapt your pace to the trail and weather conditions.
