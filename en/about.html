Who we are

Born here. Inspired by the mountains.

Imsfrane Cathédrale is a local adventure-tourism initiative rooted in the mountains around Imsfrane, Tilouguite and the Ahansal valley. Our connection to this land shapes the way we welcome visitors and share its landscapes.

From river journeys to mountain trails, we value genuine encounters, small details and experiences adapted to the season and local conditions.

OUR HOME

A place worth discovering

The cliffs of Msfrane, the Ahansal river and the mountain villages of the High Atlas are more than a backdrop: they are the heart of every journey.

THE PEOPLE

A team close to nature
