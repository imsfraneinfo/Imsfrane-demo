BUILT FOR PADDLERS

You paddle. We handle the rest.

For international whitewater kayakers, clubs and groups travelling with their own boats and equipment. No rigid itineraries: together we build a trip around your experience, goals and current conditions.

Every descent is subject to river levels, weather, access and the group's technical ability.
WHAT WE CAN ARRANGE

Everything around the paddling.

River shuttles & transport

Transport for paddlers and their kayaks between accommodation, put-ins and take-outs.

Airport pickups

TWO RIVERS, ONE REGION

Meet the Ahansal and Melloul.

Two distinctive High Atlas river environments. Exact sections, access and difficulty are decided after checking current water and safety conditions.

01 · OUED AHANSAL

Ahansal River

Mountain valleys, scenic gorges and memorable river landscapes. We can coordinate access, vehicle shuttles, camp stages and local support.

02 · OUED MELLOUL

Melloul River

Another compelling waterway in the region. The sections and logistics are selected according to flows, paddler experience and access.

WHEN TO TRAVEL

Follow the flow, not just the calendar.

The usual whitewater season runs from March to June. In some years July can also work, depending on water levels and rainfall. No dates guarantee runnable flows: we check current conditions before confirming plans.

July is conditional on water levels. Outside the usual season, ask us what may be possible—no paddling is guaranteed.

BEFORE YOU COME

Plan your river trip well.

We start by understanding your group, your equipment and the river conditions so the logistics fit the adventure.

Your experience

Tell us about your whitewater skills and group independence. We never promise a fixed river grade: difficulty changes with section and flow.

Your equipment

Each paddler brings their own kayak, paddle, helmet, buoyancy aid, protective equipment and other suitable personal gear.

Weather & river safety

Flows, weather and access can change quickly. A section can be altered or cancelled if conditions are unsuitable.

Travel logistics

Share your dates, number of kayaks, airport and transport needs, meal plans and accommodation preferences for a tailored quote.

This service is for kayakers bringing their own equipment. An on-water guide is optional and subject to availability. We never confirm a river run before checking the conditions.

GOOD TO KNOW

Answers for visiting paddlers.

Can a professional kayak guide paddle with us?

Yes, we can arrange an experienced in-water kayaking guide when available. Because our team is small, please ask early: availability must be confirmed for your dates. This service is not automatically included.

Do you provide kayaks?

No. This service is intended for paddlers who bring their own kayaks and personal equipment.

Can we paddle both rivers?

Potentially, depending on the group's experience, length of stay, water levels, weather and safety conditions.

Can you shuttle boats and pick us up in Marrakech?

Yes. We can organize paddler and kayak transport locally, along with airport transfers from Marrakech and other locations by arrangement.

How do we receive a quote?

Tell us your dates, group size, trip duration and services you need. We will prepare a personalized proposal.

Do I need to bring my own kayak?

Yes. This service is designed for paddlers, clubs and groups travelling with their own kayaks and equipment. It is a local logistics service; kayak rental is not advertised as part of the offer.

Can you arrange airport transfers and river shuttles?

We can arrange Marrakech airport transfers, local transport for paddlers and kayaks, meals and bivouacs around the agreed itinerary. An in-water guide is subject to availability. Ask for an itemised programme and quote.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Include your dates, number of paddlers and boats, experience, preferred river sections and arrival airport. The usual season is March to June; runnable sections depend on water levels and access.
