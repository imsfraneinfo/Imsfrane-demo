IMSFRANE · CENTRAL HIGH ATLAS

A weekend in Imsfrane

Three days of mountains, nature, camping and adventure

850 MADper person
Explore the programme ↓
3 daysFriday to Sunday
2 nightsTents beside the Ahansal River
850 MADPer person
14 participantsMaximum group size

Discover Morocco’s central High Atlas on a group trip to Msfrane. Camp beside the Ahansal River, hike to the Cathedral summit, explore rocky gorges and waterfalls, and share evenings at camp surrounded by nature.

Casablanca → Rabat → Beni Mellal → Msfrane

Msfrane · Tilouguite commune · Azilal province

Day 1

On Friday, we leave Casablanca at 18:00, travelling through Rabat and Beni Mellal towards Msfrane in the Atlas Mountains. We stop for dinner along the way, before reaching Msfrane.

We arrive in Msfrane at night and head to the campsite beside the Ahansal River. We meet the other participants, arrange sleeping places and settle into the tents in the night-time atmosphere of the camp.

We spend our first night in the tents and rest after the journey, ready for the next morning’s hike.

Day 2

At 07:30, we wake up and have breakfast at camp. At 08:00, we set off on foot towards the summit of Msfrane, known as the Cathedral.

The ascent takes approximately two to three hours, depending on the group’s pace. We follow mountain trails, stopping along the way to rest, take photographs and enjoy panoramic views of the mountains and valleys.

On reaching Msfrane’s summit, at around 1868 metres, we take time to rest and enjoy the view. We take souvenir photographs beside the altitude sign before starting the descent back to camp.

Back at camp, we have lunch and take some time to relax beside the Ahansal River. It is a chance to recover after the walk and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.

In the evening, we gather around the campfire, share dinner and enjoy time together before our second night in the tents.

Local cultural entertainment, such as Ahidous, may be arranged if the group agrees and it is available. This activity is not confirmed in advance.

Day 3

Sunday begins with breakfast before we head out to explore the area. We visit the rocky gorges through which the river flows, enjoying the geological formations and natural passages, with time for photographs.

We continue towards Oukhodene waterfalls, where water flows between rocks and trees. We stop to discover this natural setting, enjoy the scenery and take photographs.

On the way back, we stop at the old truck at the foot of Mount Msfrane. A familiar landmark for visitors, it is a place for souvenir photographs with the mountain in the background.

After the visits, we have lunch and prepare for the return journey. We travel through Beni Mellal and Rabat to Casablanca, bringing three days of mountains, river scenery and camping to a close.

Transport, meals, camping equipment and the services included in the price will be confirmed when booking. No specific departure date has been announced for this trip.
