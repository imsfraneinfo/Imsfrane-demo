Privacy and cookie policy

How we handle enquiries, account information, cookies and advertising on Imsfrane Cathédrale.

Updated: 14 October 2026

Website operator and contact

The website imsfrane.com operates under the name Imsfrane Cathédrale in Tilouguite, Azilal Province, Morocco. For any personal data questions, email imsfraneinfo@gmail.com.

Information we receive

When you enquire or request an activity, we may receive your name, telephone number, email, preferred dates and message. If you use member account features, we may store your email, optional profile information, favourites, orders and conversations. This information helps us respond to enquiries, arrange requested services and operate accounts.

Cookies and third-party services

The site and service providers may use cookies, identifiers or local storage for login, preferences and service functions. Some account features use Supabase for authentication and data storage. When you follow WhatsApp or another external link, that service's own privacy terms also apply.

Google AdSense advertising

Some pages integrate Google AdSense. Where ads are served, Google and other third-party vendors may place or read cookies, use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to serve and measure ads. Vendors may use information about visits to this site or other sites to personalise advertising, where permitted and subject to relevant consent. Read how Google uses partner-site data, manage personalised ads in Google My Ad Center, or explore other advertising opt-out choices.

Consent preferences

Visitors in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland must be offered required advertising and cookie choices through a certified consent management platform (CMP), to manage the applicable choices. Simply reading this policy does not provide consent.

Retention and your rights

Information is held for as long as needed to deal with requests, maintain accounts and meet applicable obligations. Technical providers may process data in other countries. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion and exercise relevant rights by contacting imsfraneinfo@gmail.com. We may update this policy when services change.
