Plan your visit to Imsfrane

Which Imsfrane hike is right for me?

Choose the summit walk for mountain views: around 4–5 hours, 668 metres of ascent and a summit at about 1,868 metres. The gorges and waterfalls walk is around 8 km in 3–4 hours. The Grand Tour combines both in a demanding 24 km day of around 8–10 hours.

Are hiking prices per person or per group?

The listed guide prices are per group: 300 MAD for the summit, 200 MAD for the gorges and waterfalls, or 400 MAD for the Grand Tour. Ask us to confirm group arrangements and any transport or accommodation separately.

Where do we meet our local guide?

The walks take place around Imsfrane and the Ahansal valley in Morocco’s High Atlas. Send your accommodation or starting point, date and group size so we can agree on a meeting place and a route suited to your group.
