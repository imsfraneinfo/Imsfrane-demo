PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Plan your stay

At the campsite

A dedicated family area, lighting at night, and car access close to the tents.

Cooking and meals

Cook your own meals using shared utensils, returning them as received. Larger groups should ideally bring their own equipment. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are available by request for at least 4 people.

Location and supplies

Imsfrane, Tilouguite commune, Azilal province, between Imi N’Ouarg and Tamga, around 90 km from Beni Mellal. Vegetables and meat can be found about 15 minutes away; Tilouguite is roughly 10 km away.

Getting here

Travel by bus to Beni Mellal, then shared taxi to Ouaouizeght (around 25 MAD), followed by transport to Imsfrane (around 30 MAD). Transport fares are indicative.

Amazigh evening

An optional evening with Ahidous music and a campfire can be arranged by agreement and shared contribution, subject to safety conditions.
