Oued Ahansal – Lifeline of the Atlas

5 February 2026

Oued Ahansal flows from Taghia and Zaouiat Ahansal before continuing toward Tilouguite and Bin El Ouidane. Along the way, it crosses valleys, gorges and villages.

Its water supports agriculture, local communities and a rich ecosystem. During spring, its stronger flow creates excellent conditions for rafting.
