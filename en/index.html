IMSFRANE CATHÉDRALE · HAUT ATLAS
MORE THAN A DESTINATION

Experience the Atlas as it truly is.

Rising to around 1,868 metres, Imsfrane’s mountain stands out for its towering rock faces. Between cliffs, rushing rivers and mountain villages, there’s so much to discover. The nickname “Cathedral” refers to the shape of the rock; it is not a religious landmark.

  • Tilouguite commune, Azilal Province
  • Mountain landscapes, gorges and nearby rivers
  • Explore with guides who know the region
