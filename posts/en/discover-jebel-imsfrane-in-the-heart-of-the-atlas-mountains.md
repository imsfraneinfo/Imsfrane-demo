Located in the Tilouguite commune of Azilal Province, Jebel Imsfrane is one of the remarkable landscapes of Morocco’s Atlas Mountains.

&nbsp;

Nicknamed “The Cathedral” because of its impressive rocky shape, the mountain rises to approximately 1,868 metres above sea level.

&nbsp;

Visitors can explore the area through guided hikes, enjoy panoramic mountain views and discover the surrounding natural landscapes.

&nbsp;

With its forests, valleys and rock formations, Imsfrane is an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.
