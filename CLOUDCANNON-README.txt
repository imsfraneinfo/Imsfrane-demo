To add a French post:
Articles FR > + Add > Nouvel article FR
Choose a filename beginning with blog- and ending in .html, then edit visually and Save.

To add an English post:
Articles EN > + Add > New English article
Choose a filename beginning with blog- and ending in .html, then edit visually and Save.

The old JSON Content/array CMS has been removed to avoid the "array input is misconfigured" error.
